The Artist

Brush Strokes by Sheniii

Brush Strokes by Sheniii is an independent art practice focused on original contemporary paintings that explore emotion, movement, atmosphere and the beauty of contrast.

From expressive silhouettes and equestrian forms to textured abstracts and atmospheric landscapes, each work is created to hold its own presence while living naturally within a space.
